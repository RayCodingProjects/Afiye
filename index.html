Where we're going

The Cliff Camp, Yahchouch — in the Keserwan hills, about 42 km north of Beirut and roughly 670 m up, on the edge of the Jabal Moussa Biosphere Reserve above the Nahr Ibrahim valley.

The hike

Two marked trails in the Jabal Moussa Biosphere Reserve — a UNESCO site — both starting from the Chouwan entrance, on our side of the valley. Walked one after the other they come to 4.6 km and a little under three hours on the move, before stops.

What it costs

  • Entry, weekdays$4 per adult, $2 under 16 — and Monday and Tuesday are weekdays
  • Entry, weekends$8 and $4, so we're on the cheaper days
  • Guide (optional)$40–$60 depending on the trail's difficulty
  • Take cashDon't count on paying by card at the entrance
  • Opening hours
☀

Start early. Trail 13 is rated difficult and climbs 400 m above the lake, and August sun here pushes the UV index to 8. Being on the canyon section before the heat builds is the difference between a good morning and a grim one.

Before you set off

  • Shoes with grip. Forest paths and a steep climb — not sandals.
  • Two litres of water each. There's nowhere to buy any once you're on the trail.
  • Stay on the marked trails. It's a protected reserve, and the canyon edge is unforgiving.
  • Tell someone at the camp which trails you're doing and when you expect to be back.
  • Turn back if it has rained. The canyon path and wet rock are a bad combination.
