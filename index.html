How it works

Three seconds
from found to informed.

No app to download, no account to remember, no battery to charge. TapFirst works the moment someone reaches for it.

  1. 01

    Wear it

    Clip TapFirst to a keyring, a band, or the back of your phone. It never needs charging — the reader powers it.

  2. 02

    They tap it

    Any modern iPhone or Android reads the tag on contact. No app, no unlocking, nothing to install first.

  3. 03

    They see what matters

    Blood type, allergies, medications, conditions and who to call — on one screen, in the order responders need it.
